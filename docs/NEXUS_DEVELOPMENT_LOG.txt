## 1. Project goal

Build a production-oriented website-monitoring SaaS with secure HTTP checks, regional monitoring from India and the USA, durable scheduling, Redis/BullMQ workers, check history, recovery from failures, subscriptions and a demonstrable deployment.

## 4. Architecture we are building

- **PostgreSQL:** durable source of truth for workspaces, monitors, regional probes, check history and dispatch intent.
- **Redis + BullMQ:** job delivery and processing; jobs may be delivered more than once.
- **Scheduler:** finds due regional probes and creates durable dispatch intent.
- **Dispatcher:** publishes pending jobs to region-specific queues and retries/reconciles publication after Redis failures.
- **Workers:** separate processes for India and USA; execute checks, persist results and update probe state.
- **Reliability rule:** at-least-once job delivery with idempotent database effects. Do not claim exactly-once execution.
- **Deployment scope:** regional check workers in India and USA; central API/database/Redis remain central services, not active-active infrastructure.

## 7. Working rules

- Never commit `.env` files, credentials, Clerk/Stripe secrets or session tokens.
- Use migrations for schema changes; do not silently edit an already-applied migration.
- Keep SQL parameterized and enforce authorization/entitlements on the server.
- Treat Redis/BullMQ as delivery infrastructure, not the durable source of truth.
- Design for retries and duplicate delivery; make database effects idempotent.
- Record what changed, what was verified, remaining risks and the next step after each milestone.
- Protect the 21 November deadline: prioritize a reliable end-to-end monitoring path over optional polish or extra features.
